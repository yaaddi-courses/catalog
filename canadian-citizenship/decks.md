# Canadian Citizenship — approved deck list

Source: the official IRCC "Discover Canada" study guide PDF (68 pages,
canada.ca/content/dam/ircc/migration/ircc/english/pdf/pub/discover.pdf).
Comprehensive coverage of the whole book, not just its "Study Questions"
appendix — that appendix (~30 questions) is folded in as practice-card
material only where it matches a pack's topic.

Known correction: the PDF's own oath text and one sample question still
say "Queen Elizabeth II" — stale. Cards use the verified current wording
(King Charles III) instead of copying the book's outdated text.
